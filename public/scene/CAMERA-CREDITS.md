# Camera on the desk

The silver-and-black camera is an original procedural Three.js interpretation of the Fujifilm X-T5, generated by `lib/scene/fujifilm-xt5.ts`. It is a simplified visual prop, not a manufacturer-supplied asset or an exact CAD replica. The attached compact prime lens is illustrative; the owner's lens model was not specified.

References inspected on 3 November 2026:

- [Fujifilm's X-T5 product page](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/en-us/products/cameras/x-t5/): body shape, silver shoulders, leather covering, three top dials, viewfinder and rear screen.
- [Fujifilm's specifications](https://fujifilm-dsc.com/en/manual/x-t5/technical_notes/spec/): body proportions.
- [Official top-view reference image](https://www.fujifilm-x.com/products-cameras-static/x-t5/assets/images/top/koes_fanc_kv_01.png).

No Fujifilm product photograph is shipped as a site texture. Branding identifies the owner's camera; this site is not affiliated with Fujifilm.

A [Sketchfab model by 3dUVpro](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/camera-fujifilm-x-t5-fec5ee9474f74a228f9230909656f6bf) was evaluated. Its indexed page reports Creative Commons Attribution, AI generation, and 407,400 triangles. No accessible authorized download was obtained, and that mesh is **not included**. The original procedural model avoids adding that large asset to the entrance.
